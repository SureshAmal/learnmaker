## Concept Explanation
This slide covers the foundational stage of **Data Collection** in Machine Learning.
*   **Definition:** It is the systematic process of gathering raw information from various sources (databases, APIs, sensors, etc.) to create a training set.
*   **Impact:** The slide highlights the "Garbage In, Garbage Out" principle—the model's ultimate accuracy is limited by the quality of the data it learns from.
*   **The Four Pillars of Good Data:**
    *   **Relevance:** The data must contain the "features" (variables) that actually influence the outcome you are trying to predict.
    *   **Quality:** Data must be accurate (free from errors) and collected ethically (respecting privacy and consent).
    *   **Quantity:** Models need a large enough sample size to identify patterns without "overfitting" to small coincidences in the data.
    *   **Diversity:** The data should represent all possible real-world situations the model might encounter, preventing bias and ensuring it works for everyone/everything.
