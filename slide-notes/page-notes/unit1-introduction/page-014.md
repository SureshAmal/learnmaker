## Concept Explanation
The slide highlights four key domains where **Supervised Learning**—a machine learning paradigm where a model is trained on labeled data (input-output pairs)—is used:

1.  **Spam Detection in Emails:** This is a **Binary Classification** problem. The model is trained on millions of emails labeled as "Spam" or "Not Spam" (Ham). It learns to identify patterns (keywords, sender reputation, link structures) to automatically filter incoming mail.
2.  **Credit Scoring:** Used by banks to determine the risk of lending money. The model analyzes historical data of past borrowers (income, debt-to-income ratio, payment history) to predict the likelihood of a new applicant defaulting. This can be a **Classification** (Approve/Reject) or **Regression** (predicting a numerical credit score) task.
3.  **Disease Diagnosis:** A critical application in healthcare. Models are trained on medical records, lab results, or imaging data (like X-rays or MRIs) labeled with specific conditions. The goal is to assist doctors in identifying diseases (e.g., "Cancer" vs. "Benign") with high accuracy.
4.  **Customer Churn Prediction:** Used by subscription-based businesses (like Netflix or telecom companies). The model analyzes customer behavior (usage frequency, support calls, payment delays) to predict which customers are likely to cancel their service ("churn"). This allows companies to offer incentives to retain those customers.
