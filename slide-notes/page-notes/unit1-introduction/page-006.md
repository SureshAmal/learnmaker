## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces how Machine Learning is applied in the medical field:

1.  **Medical Image Analysis:** This utilizes Computer Vision (a subset of ML). Algorithms are trained on thousands of labeled medical images (X-rays, MRIs) to recognize patterns associated with pathologies, such as tumors, that might be subtle or easily missed by the human eye.
2.  **Disease Prediction:** This involves Predictive Analytics. By analyzing historical patient data (such as blood pressure, glucose levels, age, and genetic markers), ML models can calculate the probability of a patient developing a chronic condition like diabetes or heart disease, allowing for early intervention.
3.  **Drug Discovery:** Traditional drug discovery is slow and expensive. ML models can simulate how different chemical compounds interact with biological targets. This allows researchers to virtually screen millions of compounds and identify the most promising candidates for new medications much faster than traditional laboratory methods.
