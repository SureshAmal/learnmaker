## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces how Machine Learning adds value to specific industries:

### 1. Agriculture
*   **Crop Yield Prediction:** ML models analyze historical weather patterns, soil quality, and past harvest data to predict future crop output. This helps farmers and governments plan for food security and economic outcomes.
*   **Pest Detection:** By using **Image Classification** (a branch of Computer Vision), ML models can identify pests or diseases from photos of crops taken by drones or smartphones. This allows for precise, localized treatment rather than blanket pesticide spraying.
*   **Soil Health Monitoring:** Sensors collect data on moisture, pH, and nutrient levels. ML algorithms process this data to provide actionable insights on when to irrigate or fertilize.

### 2. Smart Devices / IoT
*   **Home Automation:** ML enables devices to "learn" over time. Instead of manual programming, a smart thermostat or lighting system observes user behavior (e.g., when you arrive home, what temperature you prefer at night) and automates these settings.
*   **Energy Consumption Optimization:** ML algorithms analyze energy usage patterns across various devices to identify waste and suggest or automatically implement energy-saving measures, such as dimming lights in unoccupied rooms or running high-energy appliances during off-peak hours.
