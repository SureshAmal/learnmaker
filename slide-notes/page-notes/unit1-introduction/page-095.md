## Concept Explanation
**Pattern Representation** is the bridge between raw data and a machine learning model. 
1.  **Data Formatting:** Raw data (like images, audio, or text) cannot be directly understood by a computer. It must be converted into a numerical format, typically a **feature vector**. This process of selecting and structuring these features is what constitutes "representation."
2.  **Impact on Performance:** The slide emphasizes that the "form of representation influences accuracy." If the chosen representation captures the essential characteristics (patterns) of the data while ignoring noise, the classifier will perform well. If the representation is poor (e.g., missing key features or including irrelevant ones), even the most advanced machine learning algorithm will struggle to achieve high accuracy. This is often summarized by the phrase "Garbage In, Garbage Out."

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Pattern representation is the method of encoding raw data into a structured format (like feature vectors) that a machine learning algorithm can process.
*   **Significance:** The quality and form of data representation are directly proportional to the predictive accuracy of a classification model.
*   **Key Takeaway:** Choosing the right features and the right way to represent them is often more important than the choice of the machine learning algorithm itself.
