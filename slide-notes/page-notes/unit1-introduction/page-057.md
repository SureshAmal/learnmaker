## Concept Explanation
**Pattern Recognition** is a subfield of machine learning and artificial intelligence that focuses on the automatic discovery of regularities in data through the use of computer algorithms. 

In the context of the fingerprint example shown:
*   **Data Acquisition:** A sensor captures the raw image of a finger.
*   **Feature Extraction:** The algorithm identifies specific "patterns" or landmarks, such as ridge endings, bifurcations (where a ridge splits), and loops. These are unique to every individual.
*   **Classification/Matching:** The system compares the extracted features of the current scan against a database of stored patterns to find a match.

Pattern recognition is not limited to images; it applies to speech (voice recognition), text (natural language processing), and numerical data (stock market trends).

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Pattern recognition is the process of identifying patterns in data using machine learning algorithms to categorize or describe that data.
*   **Key Example:** Biometrics (fingerprint, iris, facial recognition) is a classic application where physical traits are converted into digital patterns for identification.
*   **Core Process:** It generally involves data acquisition, preprocessing (cleaning data), feature extraction (finding the important parts), and classification (deciding what the pattern represents).
*   **Relationship to ML:** It is a fundamental component of supervised learning (where patterns are mapped to known labels) and unsupervised learning (where the system finds hidden patterns on its own).
