## Concept Explanation
The slide explains the role of Machine Learning in the evolution of the automotive industry:
*   **Perception:** This is the "eyes" of the vehicle. ML algorithms (often computer vision models) process data from cameras, LiDAR, and radar to identify objects like pedestrians, other vehicles, and traffic signs.
*   **Decision-Making:** Once the environment is perceived, ML models help the car decide what to do next—such as braking, accelerating, or steering—to navigate safely.
*   **Self-Driving Cars:** These are vehicles capable of sensing their environment and moving safely with little or no human input, powered heavily by deep learning.
*   **Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS):** These are electronic systems that help the vehicle driver while driving or during parking. Examples include:
    *   **Lane-keeping:** Automatically steering the car back into its lane if it starts to drift.
    *   **Adaptive Cruise Control:** Automatically adjusting the vehicle speed to maintain a safe distance from vehicles ahead.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Perception vs. Decision-Making:** Be able to explain that ML handles both the "seeing" (perception) and the "thinking" (decision-making) in autonomous vehicles.
*   **Examples of Perception:** Recognizing pedestrians, cyclists, traffic lights, and road signs.
*   **Examples of ADAS:** Lane-keeping assist, adaptive cruise control, automatic emergency braking.
*   **Industry Examples:** Tesla (Autopilot/FSD) and Amazon (Zoox or delivery robotics) are key players using ML in this space.
