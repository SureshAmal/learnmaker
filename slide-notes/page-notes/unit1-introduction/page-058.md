## Concept Explanation
Pattern recognition is a core concept in both psychology and computer science (specifically Machine Learning).

*   **The Process:** It is described as both **automated** (algorithmic, performed by software) and **cognitive** (biological, performed by the human brain). This suggests that machines are essentially trying to mimic a natural human capability.
*   **The Target:** The process looks for three specific things in data:
    *   **Recurring Structures:** Repeating shapes, sequences, or arrangements (e.g., the arrangement of pixels that form a face).
    *   **Trends:** General directions in which something is developing or changing over time (e.g., a rising stock price).
    *   **Regularities:** Consistent behaviors or rules that data follows (e.g., grammar in a language).
*   **The Utility:** Why do we do this?
    *   **Categorization:** To sort data into meaningful groups (e.g., spam vs. not spam).
    *   **Prediction:** To use historical patterns to guess what will happen next.
    *   **Learning:** To acquire knowledge from the environment by internalizing these patterns to improve future performance.
