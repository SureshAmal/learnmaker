## Concept Explanation
The slide highlights the versatility of Machine Learning, particularly **Supervised Learning** and **Classification**:

1.  **Email Spam Detection:** A binary classification task where the model analyzes the content and metadata of an email to label it as "Spam" or "Ham" (Not Spam).
2.  **Image Classification:** The process of assigning a label or category to an entire image (e.g., identifying if a photo contains a "cat," "dog," or "car").
3.  **Speech Recognition:** Converting spoken language into text. This involves processing audio signals and mapping them to phonemes and words.
4.  **Medical Diagnosis:** Using patient data (symptoms, lab results, imaging) to predict the presence of a disease. This helps doctors make more accurate and faster decisions.
5.  **Text Classification:** Categorizing text into organized groups. Examples include sentiment analysis (positive/negative reviews) or news categorization (sports, politics, tech).
6.  **Fraud Detection:** Identifying suspicious patterns in financial transactions to prevent unauthorized activity. This is often a mix of classification and anomaly detection.
