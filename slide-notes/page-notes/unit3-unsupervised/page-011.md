## Concept Explanation
**Statistical Learning Theory (SLT)** is the field of study that combines statistics and functional analysis to provide a formal framework for machine learning. 
*   **Theoretical Framework:** Instead of just looking at specific algorithms (like a Decision Tree or a Neural Network), SLT looks at the general problem of finding a predictive function from a set of data.
*   **Mathematical Foundations:** It provides the "why" behind the "how." It uses probability theory to quantify the risk of a model making an error.
*   **Generalization Ability:** This is perhaps the most critical concept in SLT. It refers to a model's ability to perform accurately on new, unseen data. SLT provides mathematical bounds (like VC dimension) to estimate how much the error on the training data might differ from the error on real-world data.
*   **Performance and Limitations:** SLT helps researchers understand the trade-offs between model complexity and the amount of training data required, helping to prevent issues like overfitting.
