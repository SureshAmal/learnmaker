## Concept Explanation
**Ensemble Learning** is a meta-approach to machine learning that seeks better predictive performance by combining the predictions from multiple models. 

1.  **Weak Learners:** These are individual models that perform slightly better than random guessing. They are often simple (e.g., shallow decision trees).
2.  **The "Wisdom of the Crowd":** The core intuition is similar to a committee of experts. While one expert might make a mistake in a specific area, the collective agreement of many experts is likely to be more accurate. 
3.  **Synergy:** By combining models that make different types of errors, the ensemble can cancel out individual errors, leading to a more robust and accurate final prediction. This often helps in reducing either **bias** (underfitting) or **variance** (overfitting).

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Ensemble learning is the process of training multiple models and combining their results to solve a single task.
*   **Weak Learner:** A model that is only slightly correlated with the true classification (performs better than random chance but not exceptionally well on its own).
*   **Core Objective:** To achieve higher accuracy and robustness than any individual constituent model could achieve alone.
*   **Key Philosophy:** A group of weak learners, when combined correctly, can function as a "strong learner."
