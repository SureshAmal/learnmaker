## Concept Explanation
**Statistical Learning Theory (SLT)** is a framework for machine learning drawing from the fields of statistics and functional analysis. While machine learning often focuses on the algorithms themselves, SLT focuses on the theoretical foundations of why these algorithms work.

Key aspects of Statistical Learning Theory include:
*   **Generalization:** Understanding how a model trained on a finite set of data will perform on previously unseen data.
*   **Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM):** The principle that a good model should minimize the error (loss) on the training data, with the hope that this also minimizes the true risk (error on the entire population).
*   **VC Dimension (Vapnik-Chervonenkis):** A measure of the capacity (complexity) of a statistical model.
*   **Consistency:** Ensuring that as the number of training samples increases, the model's error converges to the best possible error.
