## Concept Explanation
**Statistical Learning Theory (SLT)** is a framework for machine learning drawing from the fields of statistics and functional analysis. While machine learning often focuses on the algorithms themselves, SLT focuses on the theoretical guarantees of those algorithms.

Key concepts typically introduced in this section include:
*   **Generalization:** The ability of a model to perform well on new, unseen data, not just the training set.
*   **Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM):** The principle of choosing a model that minimizes the error on the training data.
*   **VC Dimension (Vapnik-Chervonenkis):** A measure of the capacity (complexity) of a statistical model.
*   **Bias-Variance Tradeoff:** The conflict in trying to simultaneously minimize these two sources of error that prevent supervised learning algorithms from generalizing beyond their training set.
