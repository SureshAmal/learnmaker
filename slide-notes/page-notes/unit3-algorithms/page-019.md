## Concept Explanation
**Ensemble Learning** is a meta-approach to machine learning that seeks better predictive performance by combining the predictions from multiple models. 

1.  **Weak Learners:** These are individual models that might not be very accurate on their own (performing only slightly better than random chance). Examples include shallow decision trees.
2.  **The "Wisdom of the Crowd":** The core philosophy is that by aggregating the opinions of many diverse models, the collective result will be more accurate and robust than any single "expert" model. 
3.  **Synergy:** Ensemble methods aim to reduce the primary sources of error in machine learning: **bias** (underfitting) and **variance** (overfitting). By combining models, the strengths of one can compensate for the weaknesses of another.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Ensemble learning is the process of training multiple models and combining their outputs to solve a single task.
*   **Weak Learner:** A model that is relatively simple and provides a prediction slightly better than random guessing.
*   **Core Advantage:** A group of models (ensemble) typically provides higher accuracy and better generalization than a single complex model.
*   **Goal:** The primary goal is to improve the overall performance (accuracy, precision, recall) and robustness of the machine learning system.
