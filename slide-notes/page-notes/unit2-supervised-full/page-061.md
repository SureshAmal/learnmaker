## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces the concept of **Regression Analysis** applications. In machine learning, regression is used when the target variable (the thing we want to predict) is a continuous numerical value.
*   **Real Estate:** The model takes independent variables (features) like square footage and location to predict the dependent variable (price).
*   **Sales/Demand:** Uses time-series data to forecast future values, helping businesses with inventory and resource planning.
*   **Finance:** Helps in understanding the correlation between macroeconomic factors (like interest rates) and market performance.
*   **Healthcare:** Regression models can predict the rate of a disease's spread or the likely recovery time for a patient based on clinical data.
*   **Marketing:** It quantifies the Return on Investment (ROI) by showing how much sales increase for every dollar spent on advertising.
