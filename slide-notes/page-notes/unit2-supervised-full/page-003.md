## Concept Explanation
The slide describes the core attributes of **Regression Analysis**:

1.  **Real-valued Output:** Unlike classification (which predicts discrete categories like "Yes/No"), regression is used when the target variable is continuous and numerical (e.g., temperature, weight, or currency).
2.  **Relationship Identification:** It allows data scientists to quantify the relationship between independent variables (features) and the dependent variable (target). It helps determine if a relationship is strong or weak and whether it is positive (both increase together) or negative (one increases as the other decreases).
3.  **Model Complexity:** Regression is versatile. It covers "Simple" models (like Simple Linear Regression with one input) and "Complex" models (like Polynomial Regression or Multiple Linear Regression with many inputs and non-linear patterns).
4.  **Applications:**
    *   **Price Prediction:** Estimating the cost of a house based on its features (size, location).
    *   **Trend Forecasting:** Predicting future values in a time series, such as stock market trends or sales growth.
    *   **Risk Scoring:** Calculating a numerical score to represent the likelihood of an event, such as a credit score for a loan applicant.
