## Concept Explanation
**Ensemble Learning** is a meta-algorithm approach where the final prediction is not the result of one single model, but the collective output of several models.

*   **Weak Learners:** These are individual models that might not be very accurate on their own (performing only slightly better than random chance). Examples include shallow decision trees.
*   **The "Wisdom of the Crowd":** The fundamental principle is that by aggregating the predictions of many different models, the individual errors (noise, bias, or variance) of one model are often canceled out by others. 
*   **Goal:** The primary objective is to achieve higher accuracy, better stability, and improved generalization compared to what any single, complex "strong" model could achieve on the same dataset.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Ensemble learning involves training multiple models and combining their results to solve a single task.
*   **Weak Learner:** A model that is only slightly correlated with the true classification (it performs better than random guessing but is not highly accurate on its own).
*   **Core Advantage:** An ensemble of models typically outperforms a single strong model because it reduces the risk of making a poor choice based on a single model's specific biases or errors.
