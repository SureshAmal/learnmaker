## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces **Regression Analysis**, a fundamental tool in machine learning and statistics.
*   **Predictive Modeling:** This refers to the process of using data and statistical algorithms to predict future outcomes based on historical data.
*   **Dependent Variable ($Y$):** Also known as the "target" or "response" variable. This is the main factor that you are trying to understand or predict (e.g., house price, temperature, sales volume).
*   **Independent Variable ($X$):** Also known as "predictors," "features," or "explanatory variables." These are the factors that you suspect have an impact on the dependent variable (e.g., square footage of a house, time of day, advertising spend).
*   **The Relationship:** Regression helps quantify how the dependent variable changes when one or more independent variables change. It essentially finds the "best fit" mathematical relationship between these variables.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition of Regression:** It is a predictive modeling technique used to estimate the relationship between variables.
*   **Dependent vs. Independent:** Be prepared to define both. The dependent variable is the outcome/target; the independent variables are the inputs/influencers.
*   **Purpose:** The primary goal is prediction and forecasting, as well as determining the strength of the relationship between variables.
*   **Context:** It is a cornerstone of supervised learning in machine learning.
