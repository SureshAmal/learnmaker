## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces the concept of **Applied Machine Learning**, specifically focusing on tasks that involve predicting continuous numerical values (Regression) or analyzing trends.

1.  **Real Estate:** Demonstrates how "features" (independent variables like square footage) are mapped to a "target" (dependent variable like price).
2.  **Sales/Demand:** Highlights **Time-Series Forecasting**, where the model looks at past patterns (seasonality, trends) to predict future needs.
3.  **Finance:** Shows how external variables (inflation, interest rates) can be used as inputs to model complex market behaviors.
4.  **Healthcare:** Illustrates the use of predictive modeling for **Clinical Decision Support**, helping doctors anticipate how a disease might evolve in a specific patient.
5.  **Marketing:** Focuses on **Attribution and ROI Analysis**, determining the mathematical relationship between marketing spend (input) and customer engagement (output).
