## Concept Explanation
The slide explains Machine Learning through three primary lenses:
1.  **Relationship to AI:** It establishes that ML is a specific "branch" or subset of the broader field of Artificial Intelligence.
2.  **The "How":** It defines the process as learning from data and identifying patterns. This is the mechanism by which the machine gains "knowledge."
3.  **The "Why" (Outcome):** The goal is to enable systems to make decisions autonomously, characterized as having "minimal human intervention."
4.  **ML vs. Traditional Programming:** The slide makes a crucial distinction. In traditional programming, a human writes specific rules (explicit programming). In ML, the algorithm is given data and "experience," and it determines the rules or patterns itself.
