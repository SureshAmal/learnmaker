## Concept Explanation
The slide highlights the versatility of Reinforcement Learning (RL) and advanced AI. The listed applications share a common theme: an agent must make a sequence of decisions in an environment to maximize a long-term goal.

*   **Game Playing:** AI like AlphaChess or AlphaGo learns by playing millions of games against itself, discovering strategies that human players might never consider.
*   **Robotics:** Used for teaching robots complex motor tasks, such as walking, grasping objects, or navigating dynamic environments through trial and error.
*   **Self-Driving Cars:** RL helps in high-level decision-making, such as when to change lanes, how to merge into traffic, and how to react to unpredictable pedestrian behavior.
*   **Traffic Signal Control:** AI can optimize the timing of traffic lights across a city grid to minimize wait times and reduce congestion based on real-time traffic flow.
*   **Recommendation Systems:** Platforms like Netflix or YouTube use these techniques to learn a user's evolving preferences over time to suggest content that maximizes engagement.
*   **Resource Management:** In data centers or power grids, AI manages cooling systems or electricity distribution to maximize efficiency and minimize costs.
