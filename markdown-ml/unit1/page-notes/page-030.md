## Concept Explanation
**Problem Definition in Machine Learning**
Before collecting data or choosing an algorithm, a practitioner must define the problem. This is often considered the most critical step in the ML pipeline.

*   **Foundation for Goals:** A well-defined problem dictates what the model is actually trying to predict (e.g., "Will a customer churn?" vs. "How much will a customer spend?").
*   **Business-Technical Alignment:** This step bridges the gap between business stakeholders (who want results like "increased revenue") and data scientists (who need technical targets like "minimizing Mean Squared Error").
*   **Scope and Success Criteria:** It involves setting boundaries on what the project will cover (scope) and deciding exactly how success will be measured (e.g., achieving 95% precision or reducing processing time by 20%).
*   **Clarity of Outcomes:** It ensures that everyone involved understands what the final "product" of the machine learning model will look like and how it will be used in a real-world application.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Why is Problem Definition the first step?** It provides the necessary foundation for determining project goals, expected outcomes, and the specific type of ML solution required.
*   **What is meant by "alignment" in Step 1?** It refers to ensuring that the technical solution being built directly addresses the underlying business needs and constraints.
*   **What three specific items should be defined during this phase?** Project objectives, project scope, and success criteria.
*   **What is the risk of a poorly framed problem?** It leads to wasted resources, building models that don't solve the actual business need, and a lack of clear metrics to judge if the project was successful.
