## Concept Explanation
**Pattern Representation** is the process of "Feature Extraction" or "Vectorization." 
1.  **The Problem:** Computers and machine learning algorithms operate on numbers (tensors, matrices, vectors). They cannot "see" a cat in a photo or "hear" a spoken word in its raw physical form.
2.  **The Solution:** Pattern representation transforms these raw inputs. For example:
    *   **Images:** Converted into a grid of pixel intensity values (RGB).
    *   **Text:** Converted into word embeddings or frequency counts (Bag of Words).
    *   **Audio:** Converted into spectrograms or frequency coefficients.
3.  **The Goal:** To distill "quantifiable properties" (features) that capture the essence of the data while removing noise, allowing the model to find patterns and make predictions.
