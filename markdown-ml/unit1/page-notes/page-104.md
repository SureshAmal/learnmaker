## Concept Explanation
**Bayes' Theorem** is a mathematical formula used to determine the conditional probability of an event based on prior knowledge of conditions that might be related to the event. 

In Machine Learning:
*   **Classification:** It is the backbone of the "Naive Bayes" classifier. It helps predict the class (category) of a data point by calculating the probability of that class given the observed features.
*   **Handling Uncertainty:** Unlike deterministic models, Bayesian models provide a probability for their predictions, allowing practitioners to understand how "sure" the model is.
*   **Small Data Efficiency:** Bayesian methods are robust when data is scarce because they can incorporate "prior" knowledge (pre-existing beliefs) to supplement the limited training data.
*   **Text Classification:** It is famously effective for Spam Filtering and Sentiment Analysis because it handles high-dimensional categorical data (like word counts) very efficiently.
