## Concept Explanation
This slide covers four fundamental pillars of ML in the modern internet era:
*   **Search Engines:** ML models (like Google's RankBrain) process billions of queries to understand intent and context, ensuring the most relevant pages appear at the top of search results.
*   **Recommendation Systems:** These use "Collaborative Filtering" or "Content-based Filtering." By analyzing a user's past history (what they watched, bought, or clicked), the system predicts and suggests new items to increase user engagement.
*   **Spam Detection:** This is a classic "Classification" problem. ML algorithms are trained on millions of emails to recognize patterns typical of spam or phishing (e.g., specific keywords, sender reputation, suspicious links) and automatically filter them.
*   **Speech Recognition & Virtual Assistants:** These utilize Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Deep Learning. The ML model converts audio waveforms into text and then parses that text to understand the user's intent to execute a command.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Name four tech-based applications of ML:** Search engines, Recommendation systems, Spam detection, and Virtual assistants.
*   **How does Netflix use ML?** It uses recommendation systems to suggest content based on individual user behavior and viewing history.
*   **What type of ML problem is spam detection?** It is primarily a classification problem where emails are categorized as "Spam" or "Not Spam" (Ham).
*   **Which companies utilize ML for search ranking?** Google and Bing are the primary examples mentioned.
*   **What is the role of ML in Virtual Assistants like Siri or Alexa?** It is used for speech recognition (converting voice to text) and understanding natural language commands.
