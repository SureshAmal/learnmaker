## Concept Explanation
The slide introduces **Ensemble Learning** through the "Wisdom of the Crowd" principle.

*   **The Problem:** Predicting a target variable (a movie's rating).
*   **Individual Models (The Friends):** In machine learning, these represent different algorithms or models trained on different features or subsets of data.
    *   **Friend 1 (Historical Data):** Represents a model looking at past trends.
    *   **Friend 2 (Textual/Sentiment Data):** Represents a model performing natural language processing on reviews.
    *   **Friend 3 (Visual/Audio Data):** Represents a model analyzing multimedia content.
*   **The Weakness:** Every individual model has "weaknesses" or bias. For example, a trailer might be misleadingly good, or past ratings might not account for a change in a director's style.
*   **The Solution (Ensemble):** By "combining their opinions" (e.g., through averaging, voting, or stacking), the errors of individual models tend to cancel each other out, resulting in a "better estimate" that is more accurate and less prone to overfitting than any single model.

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Definition:** Ensemble learning is a machine learning technique where multiple models (often called "base learners") are combined to solve the same problem and improve overall performance.
*   **Core Benefit:** It reduces the variance and bias of individual models, leading to higher predictive accuracy.
*   **Analogy Components:**
    *   **Individual Friends:** Base learners / Weak learners.
    *   **Different Information Sources:** Feature diversity or different data subsets.
    *   **Combining Opinions:** Aggregation methods like Bagging, Boosting, or Stacking.
*   **Key Takeaway:** The collective intelligence of a diverse group of models is generally superior to the intelligence of the single best model in that group.
