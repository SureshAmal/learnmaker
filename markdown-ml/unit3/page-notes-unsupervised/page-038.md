## Concept Explanation
The slide illustrates the practical application of **Association Rule Mining**. This is a rule-based machine learning method for discovering interesting relations between variables in large databases.

1.  **Financial Services (Cross-selling):** By analyzing transaction data, banks can find associations. For example, if a customer frequently spends money at high-end clothing retailers and airports, the system might trigger an association rule to offer them a "Premium Travel Rewards" credit card.
2.  **Travel & Hospitality (Bundling):** This is a classic "Market Basket" example. If data shows that 70% of people who book a flight to a specific destination also book a hotel within 24 hours, the company can create a "Flight + Hotel" bundle to simplify the user experience and increase sales.
3.  **Health & Fitness (Recommendation Systems):** By tracking user logs (e.g., "User logged a heavy weightlifting session"), the system can use association rules to suggest complementary products (e.g., "Users who do heavy workouts often buy Whey Protein").

## Exam / Viva Points
*   **Identify the core technique:** These examples all represent **Association Rule Mining** or **Market Basket Analysis**.
*   **Key Objective:** The goal in all three cases is to find "if-then" relationships (e.g., If a user buys X, they are likely to buy Y).
*   **Business Value:** Mention that these techniques are used for **Personalization**, **Product Bundling**, and **Cross-selling** to increase revenue and customer retention.
*   **Example to remember:** "Flight + Hotel" bundling is the most intuitive example of finding commonly purchased services together.
